💖 What are Vowels? 💖

🎈 Vowels are very special letters - they make a sound every time.

😺 A, E, I, O, U are the vowels! 😺

📖 Short: A says /a/ - Apple 🍎

📖 Long: A says /ay/ - Cake 🍰

📖 Diphthong: Two vowels together - Rain, Moon 🌧️🌙
